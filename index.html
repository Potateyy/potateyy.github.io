About Me

👋 Who I Am

Hi, I'm Potateyy, a Discord freelancer with a passion for creating organized, professional, and enjoyable communities. Over the years, I've worked with servers of all sizes and developed experience in community management, moderation, staffing, and server setup. I enjoy helping communities grow by providing reliable systems and creating environments where members and staff can thrive.

📚 My Experience

I've held leadership and moderation positions across a variety of communities, ranging from small SMPs to servers with over 50,000 members. Through these experiences, I've gained valuable knowledge in managing teams, handling moderation, improving member engagement, and maintaining professional standards that contribute to healthy and successful communities.

⚙️ What I Do

I specialize in Discord setup, community management, and staffing systems designed to fit each server's needs. Whether it's creating organized channel layouts, managing staff teams, developing moderation systems, or improving engagement, my goal is to provide communities with the tools and structure needed for long-term success.

🎯 My Goal

My goal is to help communities reach their full potential by providing professional services and reliable leadership. I believe that strong organization, effective communication, and a positive environment are the foundations of every successful server, and I strive to ensure every project I work on reflects those values.

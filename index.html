About Me

👋 Who I Am

Hi, I'm Potateyy, a Discord freelancer passionate about helping communities create professional, organized, and enjoyable environments for their members. I enjoy turning ideas into thriving communities and strive to provide high-quality work tailored to each server's needs. My goal is to help server owners build communities that members are proud to be a part of.

📚 My Experience

I have gained experience through several leadership and moderation positions, including founding SparkleSMP and previously founding ShatterMC, owning RiftSMP, and serving as a Senior Moderator for SMP Finder with over 50,000 members. These roles have allowed me to develop skills in leadership, staff management, moderation, and community growth. Working with communities of different sizes has given me a strong understanding of what makes a server successful.

⚙️ What I Offer

I specialize in Discord setup, server management, staffing systems, and French translation. From creating channel structures and configuring bots to designing staff systems and maintaining communities, I focus on providing professional and reliable solutions. My goal is to make communities easier to manage while creating the best possible experience for members.

🎯 My Goal

My goal is to help server owners turn their ideas into successful and welcoming communities. I believe that strong organization, reliable management, and positive member experiences are the foundations of any great server. No matter the size of the project, I strive to deliver high-quality work and help communities reach their full potential.

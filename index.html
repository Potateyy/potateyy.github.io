About Me

👋 Who I Am

Hi, I'm Potateyy, a passionate Discord freelancer dedicated to helping communities create organized, welcoming, and professional environments for their members. I enjoy turning ideas into thriving communities and believe that every successful server starts with a strong foundation and a clear vision. Whether it's a small group of friends or a large public network, I strive to provide solutions that improve both management and member experience. Outside of simply creating channels and configuring bots, I enjoy working closely with community owners to understand their goals and help bring them to life. I take pride in providing high-quality work, maintaining good communication, and ensuring that every project I work on is tailored to the unique needs of the community. My goal is not just to create servers, but to help build communities that people genuinely enjoy being a part of.

📚 My Experience

Over the years, I've had the opportunity to gain experience in several leadership and moderation positions across different communities. I currently serve as the Founder of SparkleSMP and have previously founded ShatterMC, owned RiftSMP, and served as a Senior Moderator for SMP Finder, a community with over 50,000 members. Through these positions, I have developed valuable skills in leadership, moderation, conflict resolution, team management, and community engagement. Working with communities of various sizes has taught me the importance of adaptability and organization. From building staff teams and creating moderation systems to managing daily operations and organizing events, I have gained a broad understanding of what it takes to maintain healthy and successful communities. These experiences have allowed me to confidently assist other server owners in creating systems that are both effective and easy to manage.

⚙️ What I Offer

I specialize in Discord setup, Discord management, staffing systems, and French translation. Whether you are starting a brand new server or looking to improve an existing one, I aim to provide solutions that make your community more organized, efficient, and enjoyable. From designing channel structures and configuring bots to creating staff applications, handbooks, and moderation systems, I focus on building professional environments that support long-term growth. In addition to technical setup, I also enjoy helping communities establish strong staff teams and management structures. I believe that organization and communication are key factors in maintaining a successful server. My experience allows me to provide services that not only improve the appearance of a community but also make it easier for owners and staff to manage day-to-day operations effectively.

🎯 My Goal

My ultimate goal is to help community owners turn their ideas into thriving and welcoming communities. I understand that every server has its own vision, and I enjoy working alongside owners to help bring that vision to life. Whether the objective is to create a friendly SMP community, a competitive gaming network, or a large public server, I am committed to providing reliable, professional, and high-quality services. I believe that successful communities are built on trust, organization, and positive experiences. Through my work, I hope to help communities establish strong foundations that encourage growth and create memorable experiences for their members. No matter the size of the project, I strive to ensure that every server I work on becomes a place where people enjoy spending their time and being part of something special.
